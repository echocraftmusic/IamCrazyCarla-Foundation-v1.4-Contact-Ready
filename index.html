Meet

Crazy Carla

Carla Simpson is an award-winning actress, comedienne and host with more than 15 years of experience in film and theatre. Her work moves confidently between drama and comedy while maintaining a reputation for professionalism and artistic integrity.

On stage and on screen, Carla brings memorable characters to life through expressive presence, strong comedic timing and an ability to connect with audiences. Her creative range includes stand-up, improv, ensemble work, singing and event hosting.

“Laughter, real talk and a whole lot of Crazy.”
